# Privacy Policy — MuteAd Tube

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

MuteAd Tube ("the app") is a simple video player developed by iamd2epak. This policy explains what the app does with your information. **In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.**

## Data we collect
**None.** The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash-reporting services. The developer does not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

## Data stored on your device
- **History** — titles, links and thumbnails of videos you opened, so you can reopen them. It stays on your device only. You can remove items or clear it any time (Home → Clear all, or Settings → Clear history). It is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Downloads** — videos or audio you choose to save are written to your device's Movies or Music folder.
- **Your video files** — the app only opens files you pick or share to it, and only to play them. It does not upload them anywhere.

## Network access
When you play or save an online video, the app connects directly to the website that hosts that video to load it and its thumbnail. That website may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, as with any browser. Their own privacy policies apply. No data passes through any server owned by the developer.

## Permissions
- **Internet / network state** — to play online videos and links.
- **Notifications** — to show download progress and playback controls.
- **Foreground service (media playback, data sync)** — to keep playing in the background and finish downloads when you leave the app.
- **Storage (Android 9 and older only)** — to save downloads to your Movies or Music folder.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
